about=Plugin for calculation of Elevation-Area-Volume (CAV/EAV) curves from a DTM raster in QGIS.
    Supports clipping by polygon layer (with single-feature selection), current map extent, or
    user-drawn rectangle on the map canvas. Results include an interactive chart and a
    Cota-Area-Volume table, which can be exported as CSV and PNG.
    Requirements: QGIS 3.16 or newer. Works on Windows, Linux and macOS.
    Author: Rodrigo Goncalves (rcghidro@gmail.com).
